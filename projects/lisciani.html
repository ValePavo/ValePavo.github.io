About the Project

An AR companion app for an educational toy product line, allowing children to unlock interactive 3D content from physical materials through image recognition. Built for iOS and Android with ARKit and ARCore, the app covers a broad product catalog — each physical item triggering a unique educational AR scene. The application was developed in Flutter with Unity embedded as the 3D and AR rendering layer.
